What is StreamFlix?

A student project built as a Netflix-style browsing demo, with real Firebase authentication and live movie/TV data from TMDB.

Why do I only see trailers, not full movies?

Full-length streaming requires paid studio licensing that isn't available for a project like this. Trailers are officially public, so they're used here to demonstrate real video-API playback.

Is my account data safe?

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. Your password is never stored or visible to the site — only your email and account creation date are shown in your profile.

Something's not loading

This app depends on the TMDB API being reachable. Try refreshing, or check your connection.
